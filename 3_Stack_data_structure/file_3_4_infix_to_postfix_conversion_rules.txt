
1. Print operands (+,-,/,*,^) as they arrive.
2. If stack is empty or contains a left parenthesis "(" then push the coming operator onto the stack.
3. If the incoming symbol is left parenthesis "(" then push it onto the stack, even when the stack already contains one or more operator.
4. If the coming symbol is right parenthesis ")" then pop and print the operators in stack until the left parenthesis "(" is found.
5. If the coming operator has higher precedence than the operator at the top of the stack than push it into the stack.
6. If the coming operator has lower precedence than the operator at the top of the stack then pop and print the operator at the top of the stack, then check the precedence against the next top of the stack operator.
8. If the coming operator has equal precedence compared to the top of the stack than check associativity rule.
9. If the equal precedence operators are plus,minus,divide or multiplication(+,-,/,*) the the associativity is (L-R), in that case to convert in postfix pop and print the current operator at the top, and then check against the next top of the stack operator.
10. After the scan is complete from left to right in case of infix to postfix conversion then pop and print the remaining operators of the stack.
